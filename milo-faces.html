Worth knowing

One character, still two engines

MiloFace and MiloReaction each carry their own copy of the geometry code and their own pose table. happy and cheer are now identical in both — the navbar took the philosophy card's wide grin — but they are still two tables that have to be kept in step by hand. One engine and one registry is the fix, whenever Milo goes into the app.

One pose nothing reaches

idle has no references left in the component. It is the anxious one, mouth curving down, and it greeted every visitor until it was swapped for content. Kept in the file in case it is ever wanted deliberately.
